1. HIGH-LEVEL EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROJECT VISION
================================================================================
SYNOVA is an Autonomous AI-powered Insurance Aggregation, Quotation, and Lifecycle 
Management Platform designed for the Indian Insurance Ecosystem (IRDAI compliant).

It solves major inefficiencies in traditional insurance distribution:
1. Eliminates manual quote comparison by sequentially aggregating real-time 
   underwriting rates from top national insurers (ICICI Lombard, ACKO, TATA AIG, 
   HDFC ERGO) in parallel.
2. Automates policy renewals using Optical Character Recognition (Vision OCR) 
   and heuristic parsing to extract schedules from legacy policy PDFs in under 3s.
3. Provides an instant digital policy issuance engine with Bank-Grade Sandbox 
   Payment Gateways and a 1-Click Digital Insurance Vault (Wallet) ledger.
4. Integrates digital First Notice of Loss (FNOL) claims submission with cashless 
   garage tracking and instant digital certificate PDF generation.

5. CORE ALGORITHMS & ACTUARIAL COMPUTATION ENGINES
================================================================================

5.1 Insured Declared Value (IDV) Calculation Engine
--------------------------------------------------
IDV is the maximum sum insured for Own Damage (OD) total loss/theft as mandated 
by the Indian Motor Tariff depreciation schedule:

Formula:
  IDV = (Manufacturer Invoice Price - Depreciation %) + (Accessories - Acc Dep %)

Depreciation Schedule Implemented:
  - Age <= 6 months:        5% depreciation
  - 6 months to 1 year:    15% depreciation
  - 1 year to 2 years:     20% depreciation
  - 2 years to 3 years:    30% depreciation
  - 3 years to 4 years:    40% depreciation
  - 4 years to 5 years:    50% depreciation
  - Above 5 years:         Mutually Agreed Value based on surveyor inspection

5.2 No Claim Bonus (NCB) Transfer & Graded Matrix
-------------------------------------------------
NCB is a reward discount on the Own Damage premium for zero claims in preceding years.
Under GR.27 of Indian Motor Tariff, NCB belongs to the policyholder and is 100% 
transferrable when switching insurers:

NCB Matrix:
  - 1 claim-free year:     20% NCB discount
  - 2 claim-free years:    25% NCB discount
  - 3 claim-free years:    35% NCB discount
  - 4 claim-free years:    45% NCB discount
  - 5+ claim-free years:   50% NCB discount (Maximum ceiling)

5.3 Multi-Insurer Dynamic Rating & Underwriting Algorithms
----------------------------------------------------------
Total Motor Premium Formulation:
  Total Premium = (Base OD Premium + Addon Premium - NCB Discount + TP Premium) * (1 + 18% GST)

1. Insurer A (ICICI Lombard):
   - Base OD Rate: 3.0% of IDV
   - Age Surcharge: Age * ₹500
   - TP Premium: Statutory ₹3,416 (for >1000cc - 1500cc private cars)

2. Insurer B (ACKO Drive):
   - Digital direct model (Zero intermediary commission): 2.5% Base OD Rate
   - Tech Discount: ₹800 flat reduction

3. Insurer C (TATA AIG):
   - Base OD Rate: 3.2% of IDV
   - Engine CC Factor: ₹1,497 * 0.75

4. Insurer D (HDFC ERGO):
   - Base OD Rate: 2.8% of IDV
   - Engine CC Factor: CC * 0.8 + Age * ₹700

5.4 Vision OCR & AI Policy Schedule Extraction Pipeline
-------------------------------------------------------
Located in `apps/automation-service/` and `apps/backend/app/services/`:
1. Document Ingestion: PDF/Image uploaded via multipart stream.
2. Rasterization: PyMuPDF converts vector PDF pages into 300 DPI high-res bitmaps.
3. Neural OCR Detection: EasyOCR runs text localization and character recognition.
4. Heuristic Regular Expression Extraction:
   - Policy Number: Regex `r'(?:policy\s*(?:no|number|#)?[:\s-]*)([A-Z0-9/-]{8,25})'`
   - Registration: Regex `r'[A-Z]{2}[-\s]?[0-9]{1,2}[-\s]?[A-Z]{1,3}[-\s]?[0-9]{4}'`
   - IDV: Regex `r'(?:IDV|Insured\s*Declared\s*Value)[^\d]*([\d,]+(?:\.\d{2})?)'`
   - NCB: Regex `r'(\b[0-5]?0%|\bNCB[^\d]*(\d{1,2})%)'`
5. Confidence Scoring: Returns confidence rating (0.0 to 1.0) and triggers fallback.

5.5 Natural Language Insurance Recommendation & Scoring Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------
Located in `natural_language_search_service.py`:
- User prompts like "Find best family health cover with OPD and maternity in Bengaluru"
- Tokenizer parses keywords against product metadata: `['maternity', 'opd', 'cashless', 'idv']`
- Multi-Attribute Utility Theory (MAUT) scoring:
  Score = (0.35 * CoverageScore) + (0.30 * PremiumValueScore) + (0.20 * Rating) + (0.15 * AddonFit)
- Returns top ranked matches with rationales.

7. COMPLETE REST API CATALOG & ROUTE BREAKDOWN
================================================================================

Authentication & Users:
- `POST /api/v1/auth/register` -> Creates new user with bcrypt password hash.
- `POST /api/v1/auth/login` -> Authenticates user and returns JWT Bearer token.
- `GET  /api/v1/users/me` -> Retrieves authenticated user profile.

Insurance Marketplace & Search:
- `GET  /api/v1/insurance/categories` -> Returns Motor, Health, and Term categories.
- `GET  /api/v1/insurance/products` -> Filtered, sorted list of products with add-ons.
- `POST /api/v1/insurance/search` -> Natural Language AI query search and scoring.

Quotation & Multi-Insurer Aggregation:
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/compare` -> Scrapes and returns live quotes from Insurers A, B, C, D.
- `POST /api/v1/quotes/calculate-motor` -> Pure mathematical rate breakdown.

Document Automation & OCR:
- `POST /api/v1/documents/extract` -> PyMuPDF + EasyOCR extraction from policy PDF.
- `POST /api/v1/documents/analyze-policy` -> Extracts NCB, IDV, and renewal savings.

Payments & Orders:
- `POST /api/v1/payments/create-order` -> Generates server-side payment order.
- `POST /api/v1/payments/verify` -> Verifies payment and automatically issues Policy.
- `GET  /api/v1/payments/{payment_id}` -> Returns order and transaction receipt.

Digital Policy Vault:
- `GET  /api/v1/policies/?customer_id={id}` -> Lists all active policies in user vault.
- `POST /api/v1/policies/` -> Directly creates and commits a policy record.
- `GET  /api/v1/policies/{policy_id}/download` -> Generates e-Policy Schedule PDF.

Claims Management (FNOL):
- `POST /api/v1/claims/` -> Logs a new First Notice of Loss (FNOL) claim.
- `GET  /api/v1/claims/customer/{customer_id}` -> Returns customer claim history.
- `GET  /api/v1/claims/{claim_id}/track` -> Real-time cashless surveyor status.

Wallet Ledger:
- `GET  /api/v1/wallet/balance` -> Returns current vault wallet balance.
- `POST /api/v1/wallet/add-funds` -> Top-up wallet and log CREDIT transaction.

9. SECURITY, AUTHENTICATION, COMPLIANCE & STATUTORY NORMS
================================================================================
1. Statutory Section 64VB of Insurance Act 1938:
   - "No risk can be assumed unless premium is received in advance or guaranteed."
   - Backend enforces atomic transactional verification before updating policy status to `active`.

2. Password Security:
   - Bcrypt with random salts and 12 hashing rounds. Plaintext is never stored.

3. Stateless API Tokens:
   - JWT with `HS256` signature algorithm, configured with expiration timestamps (`exp`).

4. CORS Middleware:
   - Configured in `main.py` with `CORSMiddleware` allowing secure origin communication.

10. TOP 35 VIVA QUESTIONS & IN-DEPTH TECHNICAL ANSWERS
================================================================================

Q1: What architecture does the Synova backend follow?
A1: An Asynchronous Microservices Architecture built with FastAPI, using Uvicorn ASGI 
    servers, SQLAlchemy ORM, and independent underwriting microservices.

Q2: Why did you choose FastAPI over Flask or Django?
A2: FastAPI supports native asynchronous request handling (`async`/`await`), has built-in 
    Pydantic schema validation, automatic OpenAPI/Swagger documentation generation, 
    and significantly higher request throughput matching NodeJS/Go.

Q3: How does the OCR pipeline extract data from policy PDFs?
A3: When a PDF is uploaded, PyMuPDF (fitz) rasterizes the pages into high-resolution images. 
    EasyOCR detects and transcribes text bounding boxes. A heuristic regex parser then 
    identifies key patterns for Policy Number, IDV, NCB%, Vehicle Registration, and Insurer.

Q4: What is IDV and how is it calculated in your backend?
A4: IDV (Insured Declared Value) is the maximum insured sum for vehicle total loss or theft. 
    It is calculated by applying the Indian Motor Tariff depreciation percentage based on 
    vehicle age to the manufacturer's listed selling price.

Q5: What is NCB and what happens to NCB when a user switches insurers?
A5: No Claim Bonus (NCB) is an earned discount on Own Damage premium (20% to 50%) for 
    claim-free policy years. Under Indian insurance law, NCB belongs to the customer, not 
    the insurer; our backend transfers the full NCB credit to the new selected insurer.

Q6: How does the parallel multi-insurer quote comparison work?
A6: The backend triggers asynchronous concurrent HTTP requests (`httpx.AsyncClient`) to 
    all 4 insurer underwriting endpoints (ports 9001 to 9004), aggregates response 
    payloads, and ranks them by lowest net premium.

Q7: What happens during the payment verification step?
A7: The endpoint `POST /api/v1/payments/verify` validates the order and signature. If valid, 
    it updates the PaymentOrder to `success`, creates a new `Policy` row with status `active`, 
    generates a `PolicyVersion` audit record, deducts wallet balance if applicable, and emits 
    a push notification.

Q8: How does the Insurance Vault (Wallet) maintain ledger integrity?
A8: Every wallet balance modification is atomic. Balance additions generate a `CREDIT` 
    transaction, while policy checkouts generate a `DEBIT` transaction with a unique 
    reference (`SYN-DEBIT-...`).

Q9: What is Section 64VB of the Insurance Act 1938?
A9: It is a statutory Indian insurance rule stating that risk commencement only occurs 
    after the insurer receives the premium. The backend enforces this by withholding policy 
    activation until payment verification succeeds.

Q10: What is an ASGI server and why is Uvicorn used?
A10: ASGI stands for Asynchronous Server Gateway Interface. Uvicorn is an ASGI web server 
     that allows FastAPI to handle thousands of concurrent I/O-bound requests asynchronously 
     on a single Python event loop.

Q11: How do you handle password security?
A11: Using `passlib` with `bcrypt`. When a user registers, their plaintext password is 
     salted and hashed with 12 rounds before storing in the database.

Q12: How are JWT tokens verified on protected endpoints?
A12: FastAPI's `Depends(oauth2_scheme)` extracts the Bearer token from the Authorization 
     header. `jose.jwt.decode` verifies the token signature using the server's `SECRET_KEY` 
     and checks token expiration (`exp`).

Q13: What is the purpose of `PolicyVersion` model?
A13: Insurance policies can undergo endorsements (address change, nominee change, renewals). 
     `PolicyVersion` stores point-in-time JSON snapshots for statutory audit trails.

Q14: How does Natural Language Policy Search work?
A14: It breaks down free-form user text into semantic tokens (e.g. maternity, OPD, zero-dep), 
     filters qualifying insurance products, and computes a multi-attribute utility score 
     ranking them from highest to lowest match.

Q15: How are First Notice of Loss (FNOL) claims processed?
A15: The user submits incident date, estimated loss, photos, and garage preference. The 
     backend creates a `Claim` record with status `submitted`, generates a unique claim ID 
     (`CLM-2026-...`), and links it to the active policy.

Q16: How do you prevent duplicate policy numbers?
A16: Policy numbers are generated with unique prefixes and cryptographically random hexadecimal 
     strings, enforced with a `UNIQUE` database index on `policies.policy_number`.

Q17: What database is used in development versus production?
A17: SQLite (`dev.db`) is used for lightweight local development, while PostgreSQL is used 
     in production for concurrency, transactional ACID compliance, and high availability.

Q18: What is Pydantic and how is it used here?
A18: Pydantic is a data validation library. All request bodies (payloads) and response models 
     are strictly typed with Pydantic schemas, preventing SQL injection and malformed input.

Q19: How is CORS handled?
A19: FastAPI `CORSMiddleware` allows explicit HTTP methods (`GET`, `POST`, `PUT`, `DELETE`), 
     headers (`Authorization`, `Content-Type`), and origins (`http://localhost:5173`, etc.).

Q20: How are PDF certificates generated on the fly?
A20: The system uses dynamic PDF generation engines (jsPDF on frontend / ReportLab on backend) 
     to render IRDAI-compliant certificates with watermarks, insurer branding, and policy details.

Q21: What are mock insurers and why are they needed?
A21: Real insurance carriers require enterprise API keys and NDA credentials. The 4 mock 
     services replicate official underwriter rating calculations for end-to-end testing.

Q22: How is the database seeded with initial data?
A22: The script `apps/backend/seed_data.py` populates the database with 40+ realistic insurance 
     products across Motor, Health, and Term Life categories, complete with pricing rules.

Q23: How do you deploy the entire platform?
A23: Using Docker Compose (`docker compose up -d --build`), which spins up all 7 microservices 
     with bridge networking, persistent volumes, and port mappings.

Q24: What is the difference between Third-Party (TP) and Own Damage (OD) motor cover?
A24: TP is mandatory legal cover for third-party injury/property damage with statutory rates. 
     OD covers accidental damage, fire, and theft to the insured vehicle itself.

Q25: What is Zero Depreciation add-on?
A25: A rider where the insurer pays full replacement cost for plastic, rubber, and glass parts 
     without deducting standard depreciation during a claim.

Q26: What is the role of `Alembic` in the project?
A26: Alembic manages schema migrations, tracking database model changes over time and 
     applying incremental upgrades/rollbacks.

Q27: How does the backend handle file uploads?
A27: Endpoints use FastAPI's `UploadFile` with `python-multipart` to stream bytes into memory 
     or save to `/app/uploads` without buffering entire files in RAM.

Q28: How does the backend calculate GST on insurance premiums?
A28: Insurance in India attracts an 18% Goods and Services Tax (statutory). The formula 
     `Total = Base * 1.18` is applied across all quotes and invoices.

Q29: How do push notifications work?
A29: The `Notification` model logs system alerts (Policy Issued, Claim Approved, Wallet Top-up) 
     accessible via `GET /api/v1/notifications/`.

Q30: What is Cashless Claim Settlement?
A30: When a policyholder repairs their car at a network garage or visits a network hospital, 
     the insurer settles bills directly with the provider without out-of-pocket expense.

Q31: What happens if an insurer gateway fails or times out?
A31: The async quotation aggregator uses individual timeouts with fallback default calculations, 
     ensuring that one failing insurer doesn't break the user's quote comparison.

Q32: How is user session maintained across browser tabs?
A32: JWT tokens are stored in browser localStorage and dispatched in HTTP `Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN>` 
     headers; window `storage` events synchronize state across active tabs.

Q33: What is the purpose of the `shared_notifications.json` file?
A33: It serves as a cross-process communication bus for asynchronous worker notifications 
     between the automation service and the main API backend.

Q34: How is Docker multi-stage build used in the frontend?
A34: Stage 1 uses `node:20-alpine` to compile the Vite React bundle. Stage 2 copies the 
     minimal `/dist` artifacts into a clean `nginx:alpine` image, reducing image size to <30MB.

Q35: What is the most innovative technical highlight of this project?
A35: The autonomous combination of Neural OCR document parsing with parallel multi-insurer 
     rate scraping, instant wallet settlement, and automated digital policy schedule issuance.
